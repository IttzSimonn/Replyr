Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 12, 2026

At Replyr, your privacy matters. This policy explains what data we collect, how we use it, and how we keep it safe. We keep things simple and honest.

1. Introduction

Replyr is committed to protecting your personal information. We only collect what we need to provide the service, and we never sell your data to third parties.

2. Data We Collect

We collect the following information when you use Replyr:

— Your authentication details (email address or login via Apple / Google) when you create an account.
— The inputs you provide in the app (such as target person, goal, and tone) to generate messages.
— Basic usage data (e.g. how often you use the app) to help us improve the product.

3. How We Use Your Data

Your data is used exclusively to:

— Generate DM messages based on your inputs.
— Manage and secure your account.
— Improve the performance and quality of the app.
— Provide support when you contact us.

4. Data Storage & Third Parties

Your account data is securely stored via Supabase. Message inputs are processed by Anthropic's AI to generate responses and are not permanently stored after delivery. We do not sell or share your data with any third parties beyond what is necessary to operate the service.

5. User Responsibility

You are responsible for the content you choose to generate and send using Replyr. Please use the app ethically and in accordance with the platform guidelines of wherever you send your messages.

6. Security

We take reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect your data, including encrypted storage and secure communications. No system is 100% secure, but we take data protection seriously.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. If we make significant changes, we'll notify you through the app or by email. Continued use of Replyr after changes means you accept the updated policy.

8. Contact

Questions or requests about your data? Reach us at:
support@usereplyr.com
